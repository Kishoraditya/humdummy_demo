How we built hum

Built on research, not on time spent.

Fewer people, fresher eyes

Endless swiping makes people more rejecting: acceptance fell about 27% across one session in a Tilburg study. So hum gives three a day, swipe with limits, and a reset when you start closing off.

Pronk & Denissen, 2020
Repair you can practise

Short online couple programs improved relationships and mood in a 742-couple trial. So hum has Fix one thing, hard-talk prep and an optional coach.

Doss et al., 2020
An AI that won’t just agree

Agreeable AI made people less willing to repair a conflict, even as they rated it higher. So Jugnu shows the other side and never picks a winner.

Cheng et al., Science 2026
Small turns, new things

Couples who stayed together turned toward small bids for attention 86% of the time. Doing new things together keeps closeness alive. So: Catch the bid, Something new, a Question ladder.

Gottman; Aron
Calm by design

Batching notifications three times a day improved attention and mood in a field trial in India. So hum sends digests, with no streaks and no points.

Fitz, Kushlev et al., 2019
Your data, minimised

Mental-health apps are the worst privacy category reviewed, and a dating-safety app leaked 13,000 ID selfies. So hum works on your phone, deletes raw files and discards selfies.
